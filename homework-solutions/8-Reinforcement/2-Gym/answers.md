# CartPole and Mountain Car

## CartPole exercise and challenges

The [executed notebook](notebook.ipynb) adapts the lesson to **Gymnasium 1.2.3**, separating `terminated` from `truncated`. It caps `CartPole-v1` at **200 steps** to match the historical lesson. The reported criterion is the lesson's mean return of at least 195 over 100 episodes, not the default longer v1 criterion.

The same [tabular learner](../qlearning.py) uses clipped bins **(3,3,8,12)**, learning rate **0.15**, discount **0.99**, and an exploration probability decreasing from **1 to 0.02**. It trains for **6,000 episodes**. Every 1,000 episodes it evaluates 25 validation seeds and copies the best Q table. Training, validation, and the final 100 test seeds occupy disjoint ranges.

| Test policy | Mean return | Minimum steps | Episodes reaching 200 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Random | 24.35 | 9 | 0/100 |
| Final-table greedy | 200.00 | 200 | 100/100 |
| Best-checkpoint greedy | 200.00 | 200 | 100/100 |
| Best-checkpoint probability sampling | 200.00 | 200 | 100/100 |

This run passes the lesson criterion. The [validation history](cartpole-validation.csv), [test episodes](cartpole-test-episodes.csv), [summary](cartpole-results.csv), [training plot](cartpole-training.png), and [rendered simulation](cartpole.gif) are included.

The challenges are measured directly: final and best tables tie here, as do sampling and greedy selection. A difference is not invented when none was observed. `Qbest` is an independent array copy rather than another reference to a mutable table. Velocity clipping bounds define the approximation, not physical limits. A time limit ends the episode, while only true termination removes the next-state Bellman term.

## Assignment — train Mountain Car

The [executed assignment](assignment.ipynb) reuses the same learner, replacing the environment with `MountainCar-v0`, using its position/velocity bounds, a **30×30** discretization, three actions, and **16,000 episodes**. Learning uses raw environment rewards without a hand-coded controller or success shaping.

| Test policy | Flag reached under 200 steps | Mean steps | Maximum steps |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Random | 0/100 | 200.00 | 200 |
| Final-table greedy | 57/100 | 178.78 | 200 |
| Best-checkpoint greedy | 100/100 | 140.44 | 162 |

The best checkpoint reaches the flag in all 100 separately seeded final tests, always under 200 steps. Its improvement over the final table shows why preserving a copy of the best validation policy matters. See the [validation history](mountaincar-validation.csv), [test episodes](mountaincar-test-episodes.csv), [summary](mountaincar-results.csv), [plot](mountaincar-training.png), [policy](mountaincar-policy.npz), and [simulation](mountaincar.gif).

The current [Gymnasium environment](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/classic_control/mountain_car/) returns -1 for each transition, including the successful one; the historical assignment describes a different final reward. Actual steps, goal position, and termination establish success here. A finite seeded evaluation does not prove success for every possible start or modified physical dynamics.

## Reproduction

Install the shared requirements in Python 3.10+ and run each notebook from this directory. Rendering uses `rgb_array` with a headless SDL configuration. The only suppressed render warning is the known third-party `pkg_resources` deprecation. Training, evaluation, plots, and GIFs are generated by the notebooks themselves.
